Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nexus TikTok Bot collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the bot:

  • Discord user ID and username
  • TikTok username (when provided for verification)
  • Server/guild ID where the bot is used
  • Submitted TikTok video links and related statistics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Verify TikTok account ownership
  • Manage Discord roles and permissions
  • Process video submissions and rewards
  • Improve the bot's functionality

3. Data Storage

Data is stored securely on the bot's server and/or database. We do not share your information with third parties except as required by law.

4. TikTok Login Kit

If you choose to verify using TikTok Login Kit, you authorize us to access basic profile information such as your TikTok username, display name, and avatar. We do not post content on your behalf or access your private data.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data by contacting the server administrator. You may also unlink your TikTok account at any time.

6. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the server administrator.
